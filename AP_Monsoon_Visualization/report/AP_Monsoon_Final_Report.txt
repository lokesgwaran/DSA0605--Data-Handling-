ANDHRA PRADESH – MONSOON DATA ANALYSIS
FINAL PROJECT REPORT

STAGE 1: RAINFALL ANALYSIS
The highest yearly average rainfall was recorded in 2025 (25.95 mm).
The lowest was recorded in 2022 (14.60 mm).
Five rainfall graphs were created.

STAGE 2: RESERVOIR STORAGE ANALYSIS
The highest average storage was recorded in 2024 (624.87 MCM).
The highest median storage was recorded in 2022 (15.68 MCM).
Three reservoir graphs were created.

STAGE 3: WIND SPEED ANALYSIS
The overall average wind speed was 10.30 km/hr.
The maximum recorded wind speed was 38 km/hr.
The highest yearly average was 16.71 km/hr in 2008.
Four wind graphs were created.

STAGE 4: EVACUATION AND RELIEF CAMP ANALYSIS
A simulated dataset was created for 10 districts.
SPSR Nellore had the highest simulated evacuation count: 6000.
Four graphs were created.

STAGE 5: COMBINED DATA ANALYSIS
Rainfall and reservoir data were compared for 2021-2024.
Pearson correlation: -0.7324.
The relationship is exploratory and does not establish causation.
Two comparison graphs were created.

STAGE 6: FINAL DASHBOARD
A combined dashboard containing four visualizations was created.

DATA LIMITATIONS
Rainfall data covers 2021-2025.
Reservoir comparisons cover 2021-2024 because 2025 has only one record.
Wind data covers 2004, 2005, 2007 and 2008 for Kurnool only.
Evacuation and relief camp figures are simulated, not official records.
Station coverage varies between years.
The results should not be interpreted as a complete statewide
assessment of Northeast Monsoon conditions during 2021-2025.

FINAL CONCLUSION
The project demonstrates how R can be used to analyse rainfall,
reservoir storage, wind speed and simulated disaster-management data.
Visualizations help identify patterns and communicate findings.
Further analysis requires consistent, verified district-level data.
